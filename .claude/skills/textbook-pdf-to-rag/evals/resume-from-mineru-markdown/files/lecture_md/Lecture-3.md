## ■ Instantaneous Velocity

The instantaneous velocity is the limit $v = \lim_{\Delta t \to 0} \Delta x / \Delta t$.

Section 1.1

EXAMPLE 3 Find $v(2)$ for $x(t) = t^2$.

## SOLUTION

Since $x'(t) = 2t$, we get $v(2) = 4$.

## 1.3 Free Fall

Near the surface of the earth every object falls with acceleration $g \approx 9.8$ m/s$^2$.

EXAMPLE 4 A stone is dropped from rest. How far does it fall in 2 s?

## SOLUTION

$$
d = \frac{1}{2} g t^2 = 19.6 \text{ m}
$$
