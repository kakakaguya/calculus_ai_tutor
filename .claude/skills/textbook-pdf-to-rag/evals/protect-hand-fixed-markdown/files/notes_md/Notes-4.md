## 2.2 Friction

Friction opposes relative motion. (Corrected by hand: OCR had read $\mu_k$ as $u_k$.)

The kinetic friction force is $f_k = \mu_k N$.

EXAMPLE 5 A 10 kg box slides with $\mu_k = 0.2$. Find the friction force.

## SOLUTION

$f_k = 0.2 \times 10 \times 9.8 = 19.6$ N.
